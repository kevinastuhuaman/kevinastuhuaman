## BCP brand (sampled from these screenshots with PIL pixel reads)

- **Primary blue (navy):** `#0D2987` — headers, headings, phone-mockup app bars, body emphasis text. Reads slightly violet-navy; BCP's official primary is in the `#002A8D`–`#11338F` family and `#0D2987` is the measured on-screen value.
- **Primary orange:** `#EE7F31` (CTA fills; `#EE8031` on the "Banca por Internet" button) — every primary action: Empezar, Continuar, Elegir, Acepto el préstamo, star ratings, text links ("aquí", "Solicitar mi préstamo"), active-tab underlines, logo chevrons.
- **Success green:** `#81C73F` — completed progress-step checks and the "Menos intereses" recommendation ribbon.
- **Background tones:** white `#FFFFFF` cards on very light blue sections `#F5F9FE` (viabcp.com) / `#ECF3FE` (info chips on Dinero al Instante). Overall airy, high-whitespace.
- **Button style:** fully-rounded (pill) filled orange buttons with white bold label; secondary = white pill with 1px gray/blue outline; selection cards use 2px blue border + blue check badge.
- **Typography feel:** BCP's custom geometric rounded sans ("Flexo"-like) — chunky friendly headings in navy, generous letterforms, sentence-case UI copy, floating-label Material-style inputs. Tone is warm/imperative second-person Spanish ("¡No lo pienses más!").
- **Reference:** BCP/Samay public brand manual on Behance (not fetched — JS-heavy page, per time-box): https://www.behance.net/gallery/177723845/MANUAL-DE-IDENTIDAD-SAMAY-BCP?locale=en_US
