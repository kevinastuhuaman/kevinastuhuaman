The ones who left got a second, quieter chance.

05 · Architecture

Every simple screen negotiates with a 40 year old core.

New small businesses were auto-rejected because the risk model had no revenue history on them. They did have government tax reports. We accepted those as proof of revenue, validated against the tax authority, and shipped it inside the existing flow instead of building a second one.

08 · Resilience

The store must hold in the storm.

El Niño floods hit Peru. We shipped proactive refinancing for affected customers. Then a term was misconfigured in production, and some customers saw the wrong numbers.

Shipped

Proactive refinancing for flood-affected customers.

Broke

A term was misconfigured in production. Incorrect charges followed.

Fixed

Tactical engineering fix within hours, with me on the product call.

Told

We contacted every affected customer before most of them noticed.

Closed

Refunds reconciled with finance and accounting. Deploy checks tightened.

Customers stayed with us because we told them before they noticed. Communication was part of the fix, not the apology.

The next customer walks in with an agent.

People already start buying decisions inside an AI instead of a storefront. A store now serves two users at once: the person, and the software acting for them. The stores that win will treat the agent as a real shopper, and keep a human approval moment where it matters.
